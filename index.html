About this collection

Combined from two datasets: 20 posts / 480 comments and 50 posts / 1,000 comments. No overlapping post or comment IDs were found.

Some post bodies are visible excerpts. Four posts have no sampled comments. This is a selected collection, not a representative survey.

English is primarily machine-translated, with selected edits. Slang and sarcasm may be imperfect. Claims and opinions belong to their original authors. Chinese source text is retained for comparison.

Direct commenter identifiers are omitted here. Source links point to the original posts. The bilingual download retains source metadata.
